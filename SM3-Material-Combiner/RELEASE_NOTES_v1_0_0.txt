SM3 Material Combiner Toolkit v1.0.0

- Split heavy material systems out of the main SM3 Blender Toolkit.
- Carries the REAL MAT database and MESH -> MAT -> TEX research workflow.
- Carries material duplicate combining and legacy local-reference migration.
- Carries resolved DDS loading, DDS/TEX conversion and atlas creation.
- Uses its own SM3 Materials panel and operator IDs so it can coexist with SM3 Blender Toolkit Lite.
